# Surface brief: the launcher (apps/launcher)

Mode: Operate. The visitor completes a task: join a friend's server, get their own copy ready, press Play.
Audience: players a host invited, many not technical, on a desktop (Linux first). Job: be in the lobby in minutes.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no game art, no platform credentials in Fenji, one fixed 1200 × 720 window with close and
minimise only, no telemetry, the neutral refusal off Linux). References: HoYoPlay, Riot Client, Battle.net.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The 焚寂 sword is the launcher's progress: a single blade whose ember light climbs as the player's own copy is
checked and made ready, so readiness is felt, not read. It refuses the category's default of key art plus a news
carousel competing with the Play button.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ground (#0c0c0f) with ink-900 side panels and hairline borders; text #f2efe9; ember (#c8431f action,
#e4572e accents) reserved for the one primary action, the active tab and the blade; gold #f2b365 for names and links;
brass sword guard. Cinzel for the wordmark and screen titles, Geist for the interface, Geist Mono for data and the
host console; 焚寂 in Noto Serif SC. Riot-clean structure: a 56 px header with tabs, a main area, a 340 px context column.

STORY: The player sees their friend's server is up and who is in the lobby, sees their copy is genuine, and presses
Play with nothing else asked of them; when something is wrong (maintenance, offline, a bad file) the screen says
what and the one thing to do.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (seal + FENJI, tabs Play · Server · Account · Client, account chip, settings, minimise, close).
Main area: the blade at left (x 150), the server's name in Cinzel 54 px at x 250, status line, the host's notice,
then PLAY (ember, 200 × 56) with "Check files" beside it and the client's verification line below. Right column:
In the lobby (Wen, Shu, Kai), Server facts, launcher version.

FORM: Ember Sword, my third grounded candidate in the safer re-roll round (the owner's choice); seed key 4e3fd862.
