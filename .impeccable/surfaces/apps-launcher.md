# Surface brief: the launcher (apps/launcher)

Mode: Operate. The visitor completes a task: join a friend's server, get their own copy ready, press Play.
Audience: players a host invited, many not technical, on a desktop (Linux first). Job: be in the lobby in minutes.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no game art, no platform credentials in Fenji, one fixed 1200 × 720 window with close and
minimise only, no telemetry, the neutral refusal off Linux). References: HoYoPlay, Riot Client, Battle.net.
The owner's redesign brief (2026-10-16): simpler, streamlined, more modern, still with character; concise words;
never plain or generic, busy, flashy or "gamer", or Discord-like. The approved screens: the redesign spec's boards.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One stage, one action. The chosen server fills the window and PLAY kindles: ember fills it while files verify
or Proton prepares, and it is alight when ready, so readiness is felt on the button the player came to press. It
refuses the category's tabs-plus-side-column dashboard and its key-art hero.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ground (#0c0c0f) that warms from the lower left (vignette-warm) as the game becomes ready; an ink-900
rail; hairlines; text #f2efe9. Ember #c8431f only for PLAY or a task's one primary action, #e4572e for the active
seal's edge and focus; gold #f2b365 for people and links. Servers are square ink seals with a 3 px state bar beneath.
Cinzel for the server's name and titles, Geist for the interface, Geist Mono for addresses and paths; the 焚 seal in
Noto Serif SC. No sword, no tabs, no side column.

STORY: The player sees their friend's server is up, who is playing and that their copy is ready, and presses PLAY.
When they can't (maintenance, offline, a changed key, a bad file), one line says why and offers the one action. Game,
Account and Settings open as popovers under their own control, and close again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a 72 px rail: the 焚 seal, each server as a 44 px ink seal over its state bar (the active one
ember-edged), then +. A 56 px top bar: the server's address in mono, faint, at left; the Game chip, the account
avatar, the gear and the window controls at right. The stage's group, centred vertically at x 144: the name in
Cinzel 64 px, "Online · 3/20 players · 24 ms", the host's one-line notice, PLAY (248 × 64, ember, lit), then the
friends' faces and names.

FORM: The owner's pinned redesign (2026-10-16): structure A (one stage, servers in a rail), chosen over a top switcher
and two trimmed tabs; the signature move "PLAY kindles", chosen over a living ember glow and the 焚寂 mark; the
identity kept and the sword removed. No roll: owner-pinned. It replaces the Ember Sword contract (seed 4e3fd862).
