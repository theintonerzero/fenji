Every screen, refined

The short words stay. Now the stage is composed around its centre, the server's name owns it at 64 px, friends sit just under PLAY, and Game, Account and Settings open as compact popovers under their own control instead of full-height sheets. Drawn at 1200 × 720, shown at 80%.

1 · Playing

The screen you'll see almost every time: your server, its state, PLAY, and who's on.

2 · When you can't play yet

PLAY stays dark and one line says why, with the one thing to do.

3 · Getting in

Your first run, an invite, signing in, and adding another server.

4 · Your game

The chip in the top bar opens this popover: your copy, ready, set up, downloading or needing repair.

5 · You, Settings and updates

Your avatar and the gear open these popovers; an update waits quietly in a corner.
